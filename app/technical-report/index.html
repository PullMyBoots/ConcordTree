Input formats

Upload an aligned DNA matrix. Choose a tree type; the number of alignment blocks determines locus handling.

01

Gene trees

One PHYLIP alignment containing at least 4 taxa. The first line gives the number of sequences and aligned sites. Each row then gives a taxon name and its sequence.

Taxon_C  ACGTTCGTACGA
Taxon_D  ACGTTCGTTCGA

03

Format requirements

Relaxed sequential, wrapped sequential, PAML name-only rows and interleaved PHYLIP are accepted. Names must be unique within a locus and separated from sequence text by whitespace. DNA IUPAC symbols and N, ?, -, . are accepted; FASTA and unaligned sequences are not accepted by the website.
